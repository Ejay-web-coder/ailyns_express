Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 20, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Ailyn's Express account and the personal information connected to it at any time, whether you signed up with email, Google or Facebook.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to zhedsjimenez@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address on your account, or tell us that address. If you signed in with Facebook or Google, tell us which one, and the email address on that account.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We will delete your data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Have an order on the way? Please wait until it has been delivered or cancelled before asking for deletion, so we can still complete it.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in details, including any link to your Google or Facebook account.
  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number and profile picture.
  • For riders: your vehicle type and plate number.
  • Your order history, delivery addresses and order status timelines.

If you were a rider, deliveries you completed stay on the customers' order records, but your name and details are removed from them.

Removing Ailyn's Express from Facebook

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove our app's access to your Facebook account:

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and websites.
  3. Find Ailyn's Express and choose Remove.

This stops Facebook from sharing your information with us in the future. It does not delete the data we already hold, so please also send us the email above.

Data saved in your browser

Your cart and last delivery address are kept in your own browser. To remove them, sign out and clear this site's data in your browser settings.

For more about how we handle your information, read our Privacy Policy.
