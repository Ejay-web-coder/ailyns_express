Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Ailyn's Express ("we", "us") delivers food and groceries in the Philippines. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website as a customer, rider or staff member, why we collect it, who can see it, and how you can have it deleted.

We handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). For any privacy question or request, email us at zhedsjimenez@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Your account

  • Your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our sign-in provider, and we can never see it.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture from that account. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your emails, contacts or files.

Rider applications

  • Your vehicle type and plate number, so we can review and approve you as a rider.

Orders

  • The items you order, prices and totals.
  • The delivery name, phone number, email, address, city, province and any delivery notes you enter.
  • If you choose to pin your drop-off on the map at checkout, the location of that pin. It is optional; you can place the order without it.
  • Your payment method (cash on delivery or GCash on arrival). We do not collect card numbers or GCash account details on this website.
  • The order's status history, such as when it was confirmed, picked up and delivered.

Ailyn AI chat

  • The questions you type into the Ailyn AI chat and the answers it gives. Our staff use them to improve the assistant and see which products people ask about.

Rider location

  • While a rider is handling an active delivery and has location sharing turned on, we save the GPS position of the rider's phone on that order, so the customer and our staff can follow the delivery. The last saved position stays on the order record. We do not track riders when they have no active delivery.

Stored on your device

  • Your browser keeps your cart, your last delivery address, your sign-in session and where delivery addresses were found on the map, so you don't have to enter or look them up again. You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser's site data. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To take, prepare and deliver your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To show you your order status and your rider's details and location.
  • To review rider applications and assign deliveries.
  • To keep sales, stock and delivery records for running the business.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Our staff (admins) can see orders, customer and rider details to run the business.
  • Riders: approved riders can see the delivery details of orders waiting for a rider so they can accept a job. Once a rider accepts your order, they see your name, phone number, delivery address, notes and the items to deliver.
  • Customers can see the name, phone number, vehicle, plate number and live location of the rider assigned to their order.

We also use these service providers, who process data for us only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Data is stored on servers in Sydney, Australia.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • OpenStreetMap services (map tiles, Nominatim address search and OSRM routing): draw the delivery map and the route between the store, the rider and the drop-off. To do that, the browser showing the map sends them map coordinates and, for orders without a drop-off pin, the delivery address. We do not send names or phone numbers.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with it.
  • Google Gemini: writes the answers in the Ailyn AI chat. When you ask a question, we send Google your question, our product list, and the order number, status, total and items of your recent orders. We do not send your name, phone number or address.
  • Unsplash: serves some product photos.

We may also share information when the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful order.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and order history for as long as your account is active. When your account is deleted, we delete your profile and your order history. If you were a rider, deliveries you completed stay on the customers' order records but are no longer linked to your name.

5. How we protect it

All traffic to this website is encrypted (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure each person can only see what they need: customers see their own orders, riders see only the jobs offered or assigned to them, and only staff can see everything. Prices, stock and order status changes are checked on our servers, not in your browser.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal information is used;
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong (you can edit your name and phone number in the app at any time);
  • object to its processing, or ask us to delete it;
  • get a copy of it in a commonly used format; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these rights, email zhedsjimenez@gmail.com. To delete your account and data, follow the steps on our Data Deletion page.

7. Children

Ailyn's Express is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders for you.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
